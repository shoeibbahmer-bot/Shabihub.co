FAQ

Before You Enquire

Do we have to hire you for the build afterward?

No — the roadmap is yours either way. Many clients execute the first pilot with us and then decide how to resource the rest.

How long does the assessment take?

Typically two to four weeks, depending on the number of processes and stakeholders in scope.

What if we don't have clean data yet?

Expected, and part of what gets assessed — data readiness is scored alongside opportunity value, not treated as a blocker.

Not Sure Where AI Actually Pays Off?

Describe your processes in a couple of sentences. We'll tell you what's worth automating first.
