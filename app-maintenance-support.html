Overview

The Retainer That Keeps The App Store Happy

Apple and Google change the rules every year. An app left unmaintained eventually gets pulled, crashes on the newest OS, or just quietly loses users. We keep it current.

  • SDK and OS compatibility upgrades before they become mandatory
  • Crash triage with root-cause fixes, not just patched symptoms
  • Performance tuning — startup time, memory, battery
  • Feature releases on a predictable monthly cadence
  • App Store and Play Store policy compliance monitoring
  • A monthly health report — crashes, performance, store standing

Before You Enquire

What if the app was built by someone else?

Common — we start with a baseline audit before onboarding any app onto the retainer, regardless of who built it originally.

How fast do you respond to a critical crash?

Priority issues are acknowledged within the retainer's agreed SLA window, typically the same business day.

Do you handle app store rejections?

Yes, including a policy compliance review before a submission goes in, to catch issues before the store does.
