FAQ

What Happens After You Send This

Who replies to my enquiry?

A technical lead from the relevant practice, not an account manager reading from a script. If your problem is not something we do well, we will say so and point you elsewhere.

How quickly can we start?

Discovery can usually begin within one to two weeks. Full delivery teams depend on current commitments — we will give you a real start date, not an optimistic one.

Will you sign an NDA before we share details?

Yes. Send yours or ask for ours, and we will have it executed before any technical discussion. Nothing about your project is referenced publicly without written approval.

Do you charge for the first consultation?

No. The first call and a high-level assessment are free. Detailed discovery, audits and architecture work are paid engagements, quoted before they start.

Prefer To Just Talk?

Call the number above, or message us on WhatsApp. A 15-minute conversation usually saves a week of email.
