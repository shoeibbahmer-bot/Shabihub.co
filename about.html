Who We Are

An AI and IT Company Built Around Delivery

Shabihub started as a small software team taking on the projects larger agencies would not touch — messy legacy integrations, half-finished ERP rollouts, apps that needed rescuing. That work taught us the same lesson repeatedly: the technology is rarely the hard part. Understanding the business is.

Today we run six practices — applied AI, web, mobile, ERP, custom software and digital marketing — under one delivery standard. We are deliberately structured so the people who scope your project are the people who build it.

  • Senior-led teams — no handing your project to juniors after signing
  • Fixed sprint demos, so progress is visible instead of reported
  • Full source access and documentation on every engagement
  • We will tell you when a cheaper solution than ours exists

Our Values

How We Behave When It's Inconvenient

Values only mean something when they cost you something. These do.

Say the real number

If a timeline is six months, we do not say four to win the deal. Estimates come with the assumptions written down.

Ship, then improve

A working slice in production beats a perfect plan in a document. We deliver something usable early and iterate against real feedback.

Own the outcome

We measure ourselves on whether your metric moved, not on whether we closed the tickets in the sprint.

No black boxes

You get the source, the architecture docs and the credentials. Nothing we build should be hostage to us staying engaged.

Respect the incumbent system

Someone built what you have for a reason. We understand it before we propose replacing any part of it.

Train the humans

Software nobody knows how to use is a failed project. Handover and training are part of scope, not an upsell.
