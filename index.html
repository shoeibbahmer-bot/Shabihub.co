FAQ

Questions We Get Asked First

How long does a typical AI project take?

A focused pilot — one workflow, one measurable outcome — usually ships in 4 to 6 weeks. Full platform work runs 3 to 6 months depending on integrations and data readiness. We scope both during discovery so the timeline is agreed before we start.

Do you work with our existing systems or replace them?

We integrate first. Most clients keep their ERP, CRM and accounting stack; we add an API layer and build on top. Replacement is only recommended when the existing system is the actual bottleneck — and we show the numbers.

Where does our data go when we use AI features?

Wherever your policy requires. We support region-pinned cloud deployments, private model endpoints and fully self-hosted open models. Prompt logs, retention windows and access controls are configurable and documented.

How do you price engagements?

Fixed price for well-defined scopes, a monthly retainer for ongoing product and support work, and a dedicated-team model for long programmes. You get a written estimate with assumptions listed before any commitment.

What happens after launch?

Every build includes a support window, monitoring, and a handover pack with documentation and source access. Most clients continue on a maintenance and optimisation retainer, but you are never locked in to keep it running.
